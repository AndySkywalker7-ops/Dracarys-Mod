# STEP 5.10 — IN-GAME TEST PLAN

1. Build the branch in GitHub Actions.
2. Spawn a medium adult BALANCED dragon.
3. Disable motion if your test environment supports it; otherwise wait for idle.
4. Capture:
   - exact left side,
   - exact right side,
   - exact front,
   - exact top,
   - exact bottom,
   - exact rear,
   - 3/4 front left,
   - 3/4 front right.
5. Repeat one side and one 3/4 view with jaw open.
6. Verify:
   - nose is narrow and short enough to read as dragon, not crocodile;
   - posterior skull is visibly wider than nose;
   - eyes sit on the sides under brow ridges;
   - no eye/horn/scale/spike floats;
   - lower jaw opens from the rear hinge;
   - upper/lower teeth remain attached during opening;
   - horns sweep backward and taper;
   - head connects to `neck_03` without a visible gap;
   - no wings/body/legs/tail changed from STEP 5.9.
7. Do not recalibrate final multipart hitboxes until the head is visually approved.
